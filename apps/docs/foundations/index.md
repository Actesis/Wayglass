# Foundations

Wayglass exists to help a person answer three questions within seconds:

1. Where am I?
2. What is happening?
3. What should I do next?

Its interfaces are instruments for awareness and action. They must remain calm, spatially stable,
accessible, responsive, and honest about uncertainty.

The governing source documents remain `VISION.md`, `DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md`, and `SPEC.md` in the
repository root.
